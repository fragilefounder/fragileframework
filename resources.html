    Manifesto

    Everyone leaves the meeting feeling heard. The decision leaves with nobody. Name the decision and who takes the next step.

    What it contains
    Four familiar Agile values, each with a distorted version and a practical correction, followed by six principles.
    Use it when
    The team has the rituals, but ownership and customer evidence remain hard to see.

  • Case files

    Playbook

    The calendar invite has an owner; its purpose does not. Ask what decision, learning or safeguard the meeting is meant to support.

    What it contains
    Twelve fictional cases show a signal, the condition keeping it alive, one change to try and what to look for afterward. Four findings connect the cases.
    Use it when
    A meeting, role, metric or board keeps running after it stops helping the team decide, learn or protect quality.

  • Team conversation

    Team Check-in

    Fourteen sticky notes. Zero changes. Same notes next sprint. Choose one change to try and check whether it helped.

    What it contains
    Five checks on clarity, ownership, honest feedback, customer connection and courage. Each offers a symptom and something to try. Not a scorecard.
    Use it when
    Before a retro or another conversation about whether the team's ways of working are helping.

    One-page worksheet. PDF only.
