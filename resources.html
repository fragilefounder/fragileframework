    Team Check-in

    What it contains
    Five questions about clarity, ownership, honest feedback, customer connection and courage, each with a symptom and what to try instead. Not a diagnosis. A conversation starter.
    Use it when
    The team wants to talk about whether its ways of working are helping or getting in the way, for example before a retro.
