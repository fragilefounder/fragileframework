Choose the right check

Two checks. Different questions.

The Readiness Check looks at one piece of work. The Field Check looks at how the team works.

Which one do you need?

If the problem is one vague ticket, a team offsite will not fix it. If the problem is the whole system, a tidier ticket will not either.

For one backlog item

Readiness Check

Is this one piece of work ready?

Looks at
One story, epic or business ask, against five fixed readiness gates.
How it works
Paste the item. Each gate is scored, and a fixed rule turns the scores into a verdict: ready, needs work, or not ready.
Format
Online tool. Experimental.

ExampleA story headed into sprint planning says "Improve the onboarding flow" and not much else. Run it through the check before the meeting to see which gates are missing.

Run the Readiness Check

Do not paste confidential data.

For a team conversation

Field Check

Are our ways of working helping, or getting in the way?

Looks at
How the team works: clarity, ownership, honest feedback, customer connection and courage.
How it works
Five questions, each marked Yes, Not yet or Unsure. There is no total and no pass mark. Not a diagnosis. A conversation starter.
Format
One-page PDF, 182 KB.

ExampleRetros keep producing the same sticky notes and nothing changes. Before the next one, everyone answers the five questions separately, then the team compares where the answers differ.
