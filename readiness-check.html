Backlog Readiness CheckDemo

Your backlog does not need grooming. It needs interrogation.

A work item gets graded on the five things that must be true before a team should start building. The point is not whether the idea is good. The point is whether it is clear enough to hand off.

0 means the gate is missing. 1 means it needs work. 2 means it is clear enough to use.
Any gate at 0 means not ready. All five at 2 means ready. No vibes laundering.
