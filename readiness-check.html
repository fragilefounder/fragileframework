5

Gates before build

A work item gets graded on five things that must be true before a team should start building. The point is not whether the idea is good. The point is whether it is clear enough to hand off without performing interpretive dance in sprint planning.

  • 0 Missing
  • 1 Needs work
  • 2 Clear enough
The standard

Five gates. One awkward mirror.

Any gate at 0 means not ready. All five at 2 means ready. Everything else is the uncomfortable middle where most roadmap promises are born.

Public diagnostic

Pick a case file.

Three sample items. Three levels of backlog maturity. One quiet accusation against the phrase “we can refine it later.”

Scoring key

The pass is earned.

A strong write-up cannot talk its way into a pass. The lowest gate tells you where the risk is hiding.

  • 0Missing. Do not start unless chaos is the delivery strategy.
  • 1Partial. Better, but still asking the team to guess.
  • 2Clear. Usable enough to build, test, and challenge.
