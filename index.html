Home Manifesto Playbook Tools Emotional Backlog About Thach

frAGILE is a parody framework for product teams who’ve seen the dark side of Agile. It’s satire with substance. Jokes with real insights underneath. We laugh, reflect, and maybe even fix one thing.

Born out of retros that went nowhere and roadmaps that went everywhere, frAGILE is both a roast and a rescue plan.

💬 Why It Resonates

  • 🤯 Think like a PM... without the burden of outcomes.
  • 📆 Daily standups where blockers are emotional.
  • 🚀 Velocity is everything. Quality is optional.
  • 🧠 Feelings are data. Burndowns are theater.
