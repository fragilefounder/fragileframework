Section II. What this is

A roast with a recovery plan.

What it looks like

A collection of jokes about sprint planning, emotional blockers, and roadmaps that went everywhere except forward.

What it is actually doing

Naming the patterns teams normalize, then replacing vague discomfort with clearer choices, ownership, and evidence.

Section III. Known symptoms

Four signals the process has become the product.

No diagnostic license. Considerable field exposure.

  1. 01

    Motion gets reported as progress. The slide is green. The outcome is missing.

  2. 02

    Ceremonies continue after their purpose has died. Attendance remains excellent.

  3. 03

    Velocity becomes a scoreboard. Customers remain strangely unmoved by the points.

  4. 04

    Alignment means everyone heard the update. Nobody made the decision.

Section IV. About the creator

Built by someone who has attended enough alignment meetings.

Thach Nguyen is a product leader who has spent enough time around roadmaps, retrospectives, and transformation programs to notice when the language has detached from the work.

frAGILE is his independent passion project: part satire, part field guide, and part refusal to pretend the meeting went well when everyone knows it did not.

Built after hours, without a steering committee. Tiny miracle. Mildly accusatory.
