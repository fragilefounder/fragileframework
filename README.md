# frAGILE Framework™

**Field notes and tools for clearer product work.**

frAGILE looks at the gap between how organizations say work gets done and what happens when requirements, ownership, and decisions are unclear. It pairs pointed observations with practical tools. The humor is aimed at systems and habits, not at people doing their best.

Written and built by [Thach Nguyen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nguyenthach), a product and business technology leader.

## Explore the site

- [Home](https://fragileframework.com/) introduces the project.
- [Readiness Check](https://fragileframework.com/readiness-check) examines one backlog item against five fixed gates and shows which decisions are still missing. Read its privacy notice before entering text, and do not submit confidential information.
- [Dictionary](https://fragileframework.com/dictionary) translates 100 familiar workplace terms. Its definitions use exaggeration to make recurring patterns easier to see.
- [Resources](https://fragileframework.com/resources) includes the Manifesto, Playbook, and team conversation tools.
- [About](https://fragileframework.com/about) explains the experience and working approach behind the project.

frAGILE is an independent project. It is not affiliated with the Agile Alliance or any official Agile certification body.
