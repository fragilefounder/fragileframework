Case intake

The emotional debt register.

Not a therapy product. Not HR. Not your Scrum Master’s next icebreaker. Mercifully.

The Emotional Backlog exists for the things teams feel but rarely file: fake demos, alignment fatigue, backlog guilt, stakeholder fog, and the tiny spiritual injuries caused by saying “quick sync” with a straight face.

Submit anonymously. Read generously. Laugh because it helps, then fix the actual system because laughter without behavior change is just a standup with better timing.

Do not paste confidential, proprietary, regulated, client, employee, or company-sensitive information. Change names. Remove details. Keep the wound, redact the org chart.

EB-01 Severity: Theater
Category / Demo séance

Shiny Demo, Hollow Soul

Dear frAGILE, I just did a demo where I pretended we had progress. The backend is not built. The design is not approved. But I smiled and clicked through Figma like it was live. I hate how good I am at this.

Reply: You are not faking success. You are faking certainty because the room rewarded the illusion. Next time, label the demo correctly: prototype, concept, or theater with clickable buttons. The truth is cheaper before the applause.
  • Root cause Roadmap cosplay
  • Suggested fix Prototype truth
EB-02 Severity: Spiral
Category / Alignment fatigue

Alignment on Alignment

Dear frAGILE, I have been in back-to-back alignment meetings for three weeks. We are aligning on alignment. The work is not moving. I do not even know what we are building anymore.

Reply: You are trapped in the Alignment Spiral, where fear of misstep outranks movement. Ask what decision is required, who owns it, and what changes after the meeting. If nobody knows, cancel the sequel.
  • Root cause Decision avoidance
  • Suggested fix Name the call
EB-03 Severity: Compost
Category / Backlog guilt

Landfill Refinement

Dear frAGILE, My backlog is a landfill. I cannot look at it. Refinement just means moving trash from one pile to another. I feel like I am failing my team.

Reply: Backlogs are not sacred scrolls. They are compost piles. Archive the noise. Find the signal. Your team needs a compass, not a hoarder with admin permissions.
  • Root cause Infinite intake
  • Suggested fix Ruthless deletion
EB-04 Severity: Executive
Category / Priority fog

Everything Is Critical

Dear frAGILE, Every stakeholder says their thing is priority one. Leadership says we need focus, then forwards twelve “small asks” with visible urgency and invisible tradeoffs.

Reply: If everything is priority one, priority is dead. Ask what gets delayed, deprioritized, or unfunded. Watch the room discover math. It is painful, but so is pretending capacity is a personality trait.
  • Root cause No tradeoffs
  • Suggested fix Public sequencing
EB-05 Severity: Invisible
Category / Dependency séance

The Dependency Has Feelings

Dear frAGILE, We committed to a date before confirming the dependency team even knew we existed. Now we are “partnering closely,” which seems to mean sending them increasingly haunted Slack messages.

Reply: A dependency is not real until another team names the owner, date, interface, risk, and escape hatch. Until then, you have a wish wearing a program-plan lanyard.
  • Root cause Assumed capacity
  • Suggested fix Signed handshake
EB-06 Severity: Seasonal
Category / Retro déjà vu

Same Sticky, New Wall

Dear frAGILE, We keep identifying the same retro themes every sprint. Communication. Clarity. Dependencies. Then we create action items, forget them, and return to the scene of the crime two weeks later.

Reply: That is not continuous improvement. That is organizational karaoke. Pick one action, assign one owner, define one visible consequence. Otherwise the retro is just group journaling with colored squares.
  • Root cause No consequence
  • Suggested fix One owner

Submit evidence

File your emotional ticket.

Anonymous submissions are welcome. Confessional specificity is encouraged. Identifying details are not. We are building satire, not a subpoena exhibit.

  • 01 Remove company, client, vendor, employee, and project names.
  • 02 Keep the pattern. Redact the people.
  • 03 Tell the truth without making Legal develop a facial twitch.

  • What happened?
  • What was supposed to happen?
  • What made it absurd?
  • What did everyone privately know?
  • What detail should be redacted?

Your story is anonymous and will not be published without review. frAGILE reserves the right to edit for clarity, safety, rhythm, and reduced PowerPoint odor.

Archive note

The feelings are not the problem.

The problem is pretending the feelings are irrational when they are often accurate readings of broken systems. Name the pattern. Laugh at the absurdity. Then change the operating behavior before the same ticket reopens next sprint wearing a fake mustache.
